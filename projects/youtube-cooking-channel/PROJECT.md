# YouTube Cooking Channel (English)

## Goal
Launch an English-language cooking channel on YouTube, break out of the new-channel
"sandbox" (low initial reach), reach YouTube Partner Program monetization
(Tier 2: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours), and accelerate growth with a
self-managed Google Ads campaign. Success = monetization enabled and a repeatable
content system producing predictable ad + affiliate revenue.

## Nature
Content strategy + market/algorithm research + paid-media planning. Not a software
project.

## Dimensions
### Essential
- Content strategy — niche selection, format mix (Shorts + long-form), publishing rhythm; the channel fails without it
- Research (algorithm & platform rules) — 2026 algorithm rewards predicted satisfaction; YPP thresholds gate monetization
- Marketing / paid media — user explicitly wants a self-run Google Ads campaign; needs budget design and targeting
- Data & analytics — CTR/retention/cost-per-subscriber thresholds drive every iteration decision
- Business & revenue model — RPM by sub-niche determines niche choice; affiliate/sponsor potential shapes content type

### Optional
- Visual identity — banner, thumbnail style system; improves CTR but can start simple
- Legal & regulatory — AdSense/tax setup for payout; becomes essential at monetization time

### Deferred
- Automation/tooling for production — trigger: publishing rhythm proves unsustainable manually
- Sponsorship outreach — trigger: channel passes ~10k subscribers with stable niche audience

## Capability gaps
- None identified yet. Research, planning, and campaign design are done with built-in
  capabilities (web search + analysis). Channel operation and ad-account actions are
  performed by the owner; Claude advises.

## Intake answers (2026-07-24)
- Content interests: Persian food, healthy/diet, cakes & desserts, breakfast — no single
  concept preferred. Resolved via pillar-testing strategy (see DECISIONS).
- Ad budget: $400/month approved.
- Equipment: camera + mobile. No presenter on camera.
- Format: **no voiceover — English text overlay + ambient/natural cooking sounds**
  ("quiet kitchen" style; a proven genre and well-suited to Shorts).
- Residence: Iran / Turkey.

## Hard constraint — country eligibility
Iran is not covered by the YouTube Partner Program, AdSense, or Google Ads (sanctions).
**The entire monetization + ads path must run through Turkey**: Google account country,
AdSense account, tax info, payout bank account, and Google Ads billing all set to
Turkey. Turkey is fully YPP-eligible. Note: Google Ads billed in Turkey adds ~20% VAT
(KDV) — a $400 media budget costs ~$480 with tax, or cap spend at ~$333 to stay at
$400 all-in. Owner must confirm they can operate Turkish banking/identity before the
YPP application (~month 4–5); not needed for filming/publishing, which can start now.
