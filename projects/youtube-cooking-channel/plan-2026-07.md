# Launch Plan — English Cooking Channel (research snapshot: July 2026)

## 1. How the YouTube algorithm works in 2026

The algorithm no longer optimizes raw view count; it ranks candidate videos by
**predicted viewer satisfaction**:

1. Satisfaction surveys now outweigh raw watch time. A 100-subscriber channel can
   out-rank a 1M-subscriber channel in the Home feed if CTR and satisfaction scores
   are better.
2. **Small-audience testing**: every new video is first shown to a small group; strong
   watch time + CTR expands it into Suggested and Shorts feeds. Zero-subscriber
   channels have a real chance.
3. YouTube actively pushes new creators in 2026 — the classic "sandbox" is weaker,
   but escaping it still requires strong early signals.
4. Consistency beats frequency: 1–2 strong-retention uploads/week outgrow daily weak
   uploads.
5. Clear topic + consistent format + predictable rhythm = easiest channel to recommend.

### Implications for a cooking channel
- Narrow the niche. Candidates by monetization value:
  - Meal-prep / healthy cooking → highest cooking RPM ($5–10); attracts meal-kit and
    nutrition-app advertisers
  - Restaurant-style / gourmet at home → $8–15 CPM (premium kitchen-appliance brands)
  - Persian/Middle-Eastern food for Western audiences → low competition, distinctive
    identity. **Recommendation: this + healthy/meal-prep angle.**
- Build skill-teaching content, not pure entertainment: it earns ~2× the CPM.

## 2. Sandbox-exit strategy (first 90 days)

| Format | Per week | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts (30–60s, hook in first 2s) | 3–5 | Subscriber engine — cooking is a top Shorts niche |
| Long-form (8–12 min full recipe) | 1–2 | Watch-hours engine for the 4,000h threshold + main revenue |

Per-video checklist:
- Design title + thumbnail before filming (hook first, video second)
- First 30 seconds: show the finished dish (payoff-first), then the steps
- Targets: CTR ≥ 4–5%; retention ≥ 50% long-form, ≥ 80% Shorts
- Accurate English captions + chapters + pinned comment with recipe link
- Weeks 1–4: iterate on ONE repeatable format (e.g. "15-Minute Persian Dinners")

Timeline:
- **Weeks 1–2**: channel setup (banner, keyword-rich About, 30s trailer), bank of 10
  Shorts, first 2 long-form videos
- **Weeks 3–8**: publish on schedule; weekly analytics review; double down on the
  format with best CTR/retention, kill the rest
- **Weeks 9–12**: start the ad campaign on the 2–3 organically proven videos only —
  never advertise an untested video

## 3. Monetization path (YPP 2026)

| Tier | Subscribers | Watch/view requirement | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Fan funding | 500 | 3,000 public watch hours (12 mo) OR 3M Shorts views (90 d) | Super Thanks/Chat, Memberships, Shopping |
| Tier 2 — Ad revenue | 1,000 | 4,000 public watch hours (12 mo) OR 10M Shorts views (90 d) | Ad revenue (primary goal) |

Side requirements: no active strikes, account ≥ 30 days old, 2-step verification,
linked AdSense account, residence in a YPP country. Review takes ~1 month.

**Critical constraint**: watch time from paid Google Ads traffic does NOT count as
valid watch time for YPP thresholds. Subscribers who organically subscribe after
seeing an ad DO count, and their later organic views count. Therefore ads =
subscriber/discovery accelerator; watch hours must be earned organically.

## 4. Google Ads campaign — design and cost estimate

2026 benchmark rates (English-speaking markets, consumer/food niche):

| Format | Cost |
|---|---|
| In-Feed (Discovery) — best for subscriber acquisition | CPV ~$0.03–0.10 |
| Skippable In-Stream | CPV ~$0.024–0.05 (consumer niches < $0.05) |
| Overall YouTube CPM | $6–18 (avg ~$11) |
| Shorts ads | CPM $2–5 — cheapest reach |

Campaign structure:
1. Primary format: **In-Feed ads** (thumbnail placement in search/feed) — a viewer who
   chooses to click is far likelier to subscribe.
2. Targeting: Tier-1 English countries (US/UK/CA/AU) + Cooking Enthusiasts / Healthy
   Eating interests + recipe keywords.
3. Advertise only 2–3 organically proven videos (retention ≥ 50%).
4. Cheap Shorts ads as a complementary awareness layer.

### Budget scenarios (goal: ~1,000 subscribers via ads + organic)

Assumptions: effective In-Feed CPV in food niche ≈ $0.04–0.08 in Tier-1; In-Feed
viewer→subscriber conversion ≈ 2–5%.

| Scenario | Monthly budget | Duration | Total | Approx. output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economy | $150–200 (~$5–7/day) | 4 mo | **~$600–800** | ~10–15k targeted views → 300–600 ad-driven subs + organic effect |
| **Balanced (recommended)** | $300–400 (~$10–13/day) | 3 mo | **~$900–1,200** | ~20–30k targeted views → 600–1,200 subs + compounding organic growth |
| Aggressive | $700–1,000 | 2 mo | **~$1,400–2,000** | ~50k+ views → fast pass of 1,000 subs; risk: organic watch hours lag behind |

**Overall estimate**: with the balanced scenario, total cash to monetization is
**~$1,000–1,500** (ads + minimal equipment) over a realistic **5–8 months**. The
bottleneck is not subscribers — it is the 4,000 organic watch hours; hence long-form
videos are the backbone and the aggressive scenario is not recommended.

### Expected revenue after monetization (cooking niche)
- Cooking RPM: $3–10 (health-angle sub-niches highest)
- Example: 100k monthly long-form views at ~$5 RPM ≈ **$500/mo** AdSense + kitchen
  affiliate/sponsor potential (typically exceeds AdSense).

## 5. Decision KPIs

| Metric | Healthy threshold | Action if below |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail CTR | ≥ 4% | Redesign thumbnail/title, A/B test |
| Long-form retention | ≥ 50% | Stronger hook, cut intro, faster editing |
| Cost per subscriber (campaign) | ≤ $1.5 | Change targeting or advertised video |
| Monthly watch-time growth | +20% MoM | Increase long-form share, serialize content |
