# Content Calendar — First 90 Days (created 2026-07-24)

Umbrella concept: **"Modern Persian Home Kitchen"** — silent-cooking format, English
text overlay, ambient kitchen sounds. Four pillars rotate for 8 weeks; analytics pick
the winners (see DECISIONS).

Name candidates (owner to pick one):

Handle/branding notes: same name across YouTube/Instagram if possible; banner tagline

## Weekly rhythm (weeks 1–8)

- 4 Shorts/week (one per pillar) — Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat, publish 5–7pm US Eastern
- 1 long-form/week (8–12 min) — Sunday, pillar rotates weekly
- Every video: hook in first 2s (finished dish first), text overlay ≤ 7 words per
  card, captions on, recipe in pinned comment + description

## Weeks 9–12 — ad campaign (after organic proof)

- Pick the 2–3 videos with CTR ≥ 4% and retention ≥ 50%; advertise ONLY those
- In-Feed campaign, $400/mo budget (decide VAT handling first — see PROJECT.md),
  targeting US/UK/CA/AU + cooking & healthy-eating interests + recipe keywords
- Weekly check: cost per subscriber ≤ $1.5, else rotate video/targeting
- Week 12 review: kill weakest pillar(s), lock channel core, rebuild calendar around winners

## Production notes for the silent format

- Sound is 50% of the format: record real kitchen audio close (sizzle, chop, pour);
  no music over cooking sounds in Shorts, light music allowed in long-form
- Text overlay style: one consistent font/color pair everywhere (brand asset);
  keep each text card ≤ 2 seconds read time
- Shoot vertical (Shorts) and horizontal (long-form) in the same session where
  possible — one dish, two outputs
- Batch-film: one filming day = 3–4 Shorts + 1 long-form
