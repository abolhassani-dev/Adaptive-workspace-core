## Diagnosis

1. **The page is dormant, not underperforming.** 0 posts in 30 days; 576 views in
   90 days against 16k followers means reach is ~1% of the follower base. This is
   not an algorithm problem or a content-quality problem yet — there is simply no
   content being distributed.
2. **Follower count is a vanity number right now.** 16k followers but ~180 accounts
   reached and ~20 interactions in 90 days → the overwhelming majority of followers
   never see the page. Long inactivity decays Instagram distribution; the follower
   base is likely partly stale/inactive.
3. **The bleed is slow but steady:** −203 net in 90d, daily deltas mostly negative.
4. **Positive signals to build on:**
   - 72.6% of views come from non-followers and 77% of views are Reels → discovery
     still works when content exists; Reels are the growth vehicle.
   - The Jun 7 spike shows posting produces immediate relative response.
   - 49 organic new followers in 30d with zero posts → residual pull exists.
5. **Audience is sharply defined:** Iranian men 25–54, evenings 9–11 PM (Iran time),
   Sat/Sun/Mon strongest. Content and timing should target exactly this until data
   says otherwise.

## Strategy implication

Phase 1 goal reframed: **reactivation before growth**. Chasing follower growth
tactics on a dormant page is pointless; the first 4 weeks are about re-establishing
consistent distribution and measuring response.

### Reactivation plan (4 weeks)
- **Cadence:** 3 Reels/week minimum (Reels = 77% of views, main discovery surface),
  posted 8:30–9:30 PM Iran time, prioritizing Sat/Sun/Mon slots.
- **Stories:** near-daily, to resurface the page for the existing 16k.
- **Content direction:** pending the niche/positioning answer from the owner
  (open question from intake — still unanswered).

### KPI baselines and 4-week targets
| KPI | Baseline (now) | Target (after 4 consistent weeks) |
|---|---|---|
| Views/week | ~45 | 10× (≥450) — low bar, base is near zero |
| Reach/week | ~15 accounts | ≥300 |
| Net followers/week | negative | ≥ 0 (stop the bleed) |
| Interactions/week | ~2 | ≥50 |

Targets are deliberately modest: the first milestone is proving distribution
responds to consistency, then raising bars from real data.
